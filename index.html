Caveats. This covers cases reported in the media and in FDA notices only, so it is not a complete list. “Fake” covers several things: counterfeit brands, unregistered imports with no Thai label, and products found to contain banned substances. Entries from the FDA monthly test results give only the month. The ฿238M Sakon Nakhon customs case covers all the trademark-infringing goods seized, not only cosmetics, and it makes up most of the total value. Brand names in counterfeit cases belong to the real companies, whose own products were not found to be unsafe. Photos © their original publishers and are linked to each source.
